## 🌍 Overview

**Corporate Nexus** is a companion utility layer designed for players of industrial colony simulations who want a *second pair of hands* in managing the chaos of a sprawling, resource-hungry settlement. Think of it as a **digital foreman's clipboard** — always open, always helpful, never in the way. Instead of drowning in spreadsheets of iron ore, copper wire, and steam output, Corporate Nexus curates that data into a clean, responsive overlay that lets you make smarter decisions in real time.

Where most tools stop at slapping a menu on the screen, Corporate Nexus reimagines the relationship between player and simulation. It treats your settlement like a living organism — one with pulses, bottlenecks, and appetite. The suite provides a **bespoke control deck** that surfaces exactly the metrics you care about, from pollution saturation curves to the throughput of your supply chain, all without interrupting the immersive rhythm of play.

This repository contains the source, documentation, and community-driven roadmap for the **Corporate Nexus** project — a distinctly separate undertaking from any existing trainer-style utilities, and one that leans heavily into *observability* and *intentional design* rather than blunt-force manipulation.

## 💡 Why Corporate Nexus Exists

Industrial simulations are magnificent — until they are not. Around the hour mark, most players discover that their sandbox has become a labyrinth of dependencies: iron plates feed steel mills, steel mills feed construction, construction stalls because of a missing polymer, and somewhere in the background a coal plant is slowly poisoning the sky. What was once a joyful experiment becomes **logistics archaeology**.

**Corporate Nexus** was born from a simple question: *what if the game told you where the friction was, before you had to dig for it?*

Instead of a blunt instrument, we deliver a **surgeon's kit**. Instead of an avalanche of options, we give you a **curated palette**. The result is a smoother, calmer, and more reflective experience for players who want to build *with intent*.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Setting up Corporate Nexus is intentionally gentle — no obscure terminal rituals, no dependency hunts, no convoluted pipelines. The onboarding flow was authored with the assumption that you want to spend your evening *playing*, not *provisioning*.

1. **Acquire the current stable build** using the macro placed at the top of this document.
2. **Extract to a folder of your choice** — anywhere writable works; the launcher is entirely self-contained.
3. **Run the launcher executable** for your operating system. The first-run wizard will detect your simulation installation path.
4. **Confirm your preferred hotkeys** in the onboarding panel. The default layout is designed to feel invisible, but every binding is remappable.
5. **Launch your simulation** and allow the companion layer to attach when prompted. A brief toast will confirm successful linkage.
6. **Explore the dashboard** at your own pace. Nothing is mandatory; every module can be toggled independently.

If anything feels unclear, the support channels below exist for exactly that reason. There are no silly questions in industrial automation.

## 🧠 Design Philosophy

Corporate Nexus follows four guiding principles:

1. **Observability over omnipotence.** We would rather show you *why* a bottleneck exists than magically erase it. Insight scales better than shortcuts.
2. **Reversibility as a right.** Every action in the suite can be undone. Experimentation without consequence is the fastest path to mastery.
3. **Quiet interfaces.** A tool that shouts at you is a tool you will uninstall. Our overlay whispers, then steps aside.
4. **Community as co-author.** Feature requests, translations, and bug reports are treated as first-class contributions — not noise to be triaged away.

## 🛠️ Technical Overview

The suite is organized into a slim native launcher and a modular overlay runtime. Each module — resources, pollution, map, creative — is loaded lazily, which keeps idle memory usage remarkably low.

- **Runtime**: Cross-platform desktop shell
- **Storage**: Local-only, SQLite-backed snapshot history
- **Interface layer**: Custom-rendered overlay compositor
- **Extensibility**: Plugin manifest with declarative capability scopes
- **Update channel**: Signed release notes; no background push

Contributors are encouraged to open an issue before proposing large architectural shifts so we can align on direction.
